# Stream-safe Music

For streaming and recording. Skate 3's soundtrack is licensed music, so streams, VODs and videos that play it can be
muted or taken down. This mod plays music that streams and videos may use instead, and shows each song's title and
artist on screen when it starts.

## Turn it on

1. Open the Skate launcher.
2. Go to **Tools** and press **Stream-safe mode**.
3. Wait while it downloads the music and gets it ready. This happens once; it can take a few minutes.
4. Play. The soundtrack is replaced by the stream-safe music.

While Stream-safe mode is on, the Custom Music mod is paused (your own music could be copyrighted). Turning Stream-safe
mode off brings the soundtrack and Custom Music back.

## Credit the artists

Every song is credited in full, even when its license does not ask for it. The launcher writes all the credits to
**CREDITS.txt** in its `stream-safe` folder. Paste them into your stream or video description.

## Good to know

- Licenses can change, and platforms' automatic matching can still flag a song by mistake. Keep the credits in your
  description; they are what you point to if a claim comes in.
- The downloaded songs stay on your PC when you turn the mode off. **Tools > Remove stream-safe songs** deletes them.
- The songs come from Free Music Archive, Internet Archive and OpenGameArt, only tracks whose artist or label states a
  CC0 or CC BY license on their own page.
