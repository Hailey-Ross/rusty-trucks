# Known issues (hails-additions)

The current known issues of the `hails-additions` branch, kept in step with the rolling
[`hails-additions` release](https://github.com/Hailey-Ross/rusty-trucks/releases/tag/hails-additions) (its notes:
[RELEASE-NOTES.md](RELEASE-NOTES.md)). Fixed items move out of this list into the change's own document.

## Known issues

- **NPC skaters:** some spin round at a few line switches; grind variety, landings, spins and grabs are not finished.
- **Pedestrians:** they walk through a prop you are holding; one model walks stiffly and one has a flat head.
- **Cars** pop in and out instead of fading with distance.
- **Carrying props:** stepping up curbs while dragging is not Skate 3's yet.
- **Loading screen:** loads started from the PC menu still show the PC menu's progress text.
- **NPC draw distance** (Esc > GRAPHICS) is a quality-of-life option, not retail; higher settings cost frame time.

## Known missing features

- **Not ported yet:** the Challenge Map's challenge rows (and the minimap's challenge icons), the zombie cheat screen,
  the pros at their spots, the Object Dropper editor, peds watching you.
- **Multiplayer for the living world:** built ready for it, no networking yet.
